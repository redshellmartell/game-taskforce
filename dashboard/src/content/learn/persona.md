---
title: What is a persona?
link: panel/README.md
---
A persona is a made-up player modelled on a real type of gamer (the strategist, the casual, the competitor, the story lover, the family player). Each has things they love, pet peeves, a way of playing, and weights for what makes a game fun to them. They are language-model roleplay anchored on research, not real people.
