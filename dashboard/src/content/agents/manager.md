The **Director** is the main Claude Code session you talk to. It runs the pipeline, hands work to the specialist agents, decides what moves forward and what gets killed, and writes the final pitch.

- **Reads:** every report the other agents produce.
- **Writes:** `pitch.md`, the one-page summary for you, and keeps `games/STATUS.md` up to date.
- **Hands off to:** you. Nothing is built, bought or published until you decide.
- The Director never contacts anyone or spends money. You stay in charge.
