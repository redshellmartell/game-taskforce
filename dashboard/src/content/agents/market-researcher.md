The **Market Researcher** is the studio's scout. Before anyone designs anything, it searches the web for what board and card games are selling, what players are asking for, and where nobody is serving them well.

- **Reads:** websites, reviews, crowdfunding pages. This is the only agent that browses the web, which is why it has search tools.
- **Writes:** `brief.md`, a short design brief that scores the idea out of 30. If the score is under 18, the Director stops the idea right there.
- **Hands off to:** the Design Studio.
