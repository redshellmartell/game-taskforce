**The Test Panel** is the studio's public test: five types of player (strategist, casual, competitor, story lover, family) who give every game a second opinion on top of the bot playtest.

Their bots play a rotation of games against each other for free, code turns the results into predicted ratings, and, only when you approve it, a small model writes each persona's in-character review. Open a persona to see who they are, what they have played, how they rate, and how well they matched real players.
