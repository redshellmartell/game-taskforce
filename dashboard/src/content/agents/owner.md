**You** are the last step. The agents research, design, test and pitch, but only you decide which games become physical prototypes.

You can step in at any stage. Use **+ New idea** at the top to push an idea of your own into the pipeline, or click any agent and open **Talk to it** to leave it a note. The dashboard saves your idea in `games/_inbox/`; then you tell the Director to process it, because the Director is the one who runs the agents.
