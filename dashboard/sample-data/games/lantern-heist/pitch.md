# Pitch: Lantern Heist

**Hook:** Outbid your rivals in the dark and steal the brightest lanterns.

## How it plays
Each round every player secretly bids one card from their hand for the lantern on offer. Bids are revealed together, the highest unique bid wins the lantern, and ties burn both cards. Collect matching lanterns for set bonuses; the player with the brightest collection after the last round wins.

## Components
- 54 cards
- 12 lantern tokens
