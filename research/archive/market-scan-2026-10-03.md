# Market scan, 2026-10-03

Budget note: owner-approved reduced scan (market-scan-1). 8 searches and 3 page reads used (all 3 reads were blocked by the network proxy: boardgamewire.com, theop.games, discover.gamefound.com). Everything below comes from search-result summaries, so treat evidence as medium-confidence. BoardGameGeek and Kickstarter pages were not opened; no BGG ratings were found (null in the bank).

## Trends
- **Small, fast card games dominate play counts.** Flip 7 (Renegade/The OP, 2024) had about 15,400 logged plays by March 2026, over 2 million sold, a Spiel des Jahres nomination and a smaller tuckbox second edition (its big box was the main complaint). Bomb Busters (2024, 2-4 co-op) followed with about 13,900 plays; LOTR: Fellowship trick-taking (Z-Man, 2025) with about 12,900.
- **Solo is now a defining mechanism.** University research of 104,000 titles (via BoardGameWire, July 2026) ranks solo play third most common component for 2020-2025 releases. Opinionated Gamers and Zatu also describe a solo boom.
- **Two-player and under-two-hour games** draw the strongest online reactions.
- **Pocket versions of big hits:** Wingspan Pocket (Stonemaier, July 2026, 1-5 players, 25-35 min, icon-based powers, double-sided cards).
- **Co-op with no "quarterback":** Sky Team, The Crew, Bomb Busters. Players want limited-communication co-op.
- **Crowdfunding:** Cyberpunk TCG (WeirdCo/CD Projekt Red) passed $13M in March 2026, the biggest tabletop campaign ever, but this is licensed-IP, out of reach for a solo creator. Gamefound reports card games fund strongly; small funded examples include Riposte! and Cell Block 18 micro card games, Tiny Library: Modern Fantasy, and a Gamefound trick-taking trio (Supercollider, Growing Groves, The Franciscan Diamond).
- **Complaints that recur:** downtime on long turns (Wyrmspan, Oath), fiddly upkeep such as sleeving, large boxes for small games, out-of-stock hits.

## Saturated, avoid
- Flip-7-style push-your-luck (already used by Duel Flip; many clones coming).
- Licensed TCGs and big-box miniatures.
- Generic engine-building with long turns.
- Standard 4-player party trick-takers (banked idea scored low on gap).

## Gaps worth pursuing
- **Exactly 3 players:** most card games assume 2 or 4; search results confirm 3 is "the awkward number".
- **Limited-communication co-op for 2-3** in under 25 minutes.
- **Simultaneous-play micro games** (no downtime) for 2-5.
- **Solo-first small-box** with a real 2-player option, in a tuckbox.
- **Cheap print-and-play-friendly designs** (pad-based, few cards) suit a solo prototyper.

## Gaps in evidence
- No BGG ratings, no Kickstarter funding for small games beyond names, no price-point data. A later scan with unblocked access should check BGG category rankings and Gamefound funding totals.
