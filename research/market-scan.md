# Market scan 2, 2026-10-03

Budget: owner-approved extended scan (market-scan-2). 9 searches used, 3 page reads attempted and all 3 blocked by the network proxy (opinionatedgamers.com, hexagamers.com, twoaveragegamers.com). BoardGameGeek, Reddit and Kickstarter were not opened. All evidence is from search-result summaries (medium to low confidence); no new BGG ratings were found (null in the bank).

## Trends
- **Card-path and tableau card games are drawing big money.** Everdell Journeys (Tycoon Games, 1-6p, 30-60 min, card paths with a traveller token) launched on Kickstarter on 8 September 2026 and reached about $446k from 3,593 backers against a $35k goal.
- **Small box, big brain.** 2026 round-ups describe tuckbox games of about 25 minutes (for example Carnuta) beating heavy boxes for table time. Opinionated Gamers' solo review (49 solo games in 8 months) picks Tenby for its small portable box and both solo and multiplayer modes.
- **Solo is established.** Friday, Onirim and One Deck Dungeon still lead Reddit-based solo lists, and Sprawlopolis (18 cards) is cited as the compact solo model.
- **Setup time is the named killer.** A 2026 essay and review-complaint results rank setup time, table space, long rulebooks and honest play times as the main barriers to getting games played.
- **Social deduction and gambling are returning**, now with traitors inside co-ops and no-elimination variants (Staked! at 6-12 players).
- **Cosy play** is a stated trend (Cozy Stickerville, low rules threshold).
- **Crowdfunding:** Gamefound took 6 of the top 10 campaigns in 2024 (Cyberpunk 2077 board game $7.6m, Lands of Evershade $7.4m); overall dollars were flat. Large totals come from licences and big boxes, which are out of reach for a solo creator.
- **Player counts:** 5-6 players is repeatedly called underserved (most games stop at 5; a June 2026 podcast episode calls six players a sweet spot). Three is the awkward count (previous scan).

## Saturated, avoid
- Flip 7-style push-your-luck (Duel Flip already covers this).
- Licensed TCGs and big-box miniatures crawlers (banked as rejected).
- Wingspan-style engine builders and card-path tableaus from big brands (Wingspan Pocket, Everdell Journeys): only enter with a clear twist.
- Generic roll-and-write and standard 4-player party trick-takers.

## Gaps worth pursuing
- **No-setup designs:** games played with a standard deck and a printed rule sheet.
- **5-6 player card games with no downtime** (simultaneous bidding, hidden traitors without elimination).
- **True two-player versions of genres that usually need more people** (hidden roles, negotiation).
- **Solo games that fit in nine cards or ten minutes**, with persistent progress between runs.
- **Partnership games for 4 and 6 with hidden or rotating teams.**

## Gaps in evidence
- No BGG category rankings, ratings or Gamefound totals for small card games; no price-point data. A scan with unblocked access should check BGG's 5-6 player and 2-player lists.
