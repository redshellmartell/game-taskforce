# Brief: Tug of Crowns

- **Working title / slug:** Tug of Crowns / `asymmetric-duel-tug-of-war`
- **Idea-bank id:** `asymmetric-duel-tug-of-war` (from scan 2026-10-03)
- **Hook:** Two rival court advisors, each with a different 20-card deck and rulebook, haul one shared crown track toward their side in a 15-minute duel.

## Target
- Players: 2 | Time: 15 min (cap 20) | Audience: ages 12+, gamers who want a quick, deep 2P filler | Complexity: 2/5

## Core mechanics and theme
- One tug-of-war track (a row of 7-9 positions, marked with cards or a single token).
- Each side has a fixed 20-card deck with its own special rules (no deck-building, no collection). Hand management: play cards to push the track, trigger side powers, or set up later turns.
- Theme: rival court advisors manipulating a throne. Example sides: the Treasurer (steady, resource-based pushes) and the Whisperer (tricky, reactive plays).

## Market gap
- Two-player focus is a named 2026 trend, and games under two hours draw the strongest reactions (boardgamequest "best of 2026 so far"; boardgamewire).
- Asymmetric 2P games are well rated but mostly heavy or board-based. Ironwood (asymmetric decks) has BGG 7.8 from about 2,000 ratings (search summary of petersengames.com list). Neuroshima: Convoy and Tug of Roar are also asymmetric duels, but with boards, auctions or longer play.
- The gap is a card-only, 40-card, 15-minute asymmetric duel. Evidence for the gap is moderate: I could not open BGG, so there are no exact counts.

## Comparables
1. **Ironwood** (BGG about 7.8): asymmetric decks and goals on a map. This game uses no map, one track and 15 minutes.
2. **Star Realms** (BGG not checked): deck-building duel. Here the decks are fixed and asymmetric, with no purchasing.
3. **Tug of Roar / TugBoats**: tug-of-war tracks in other formats. This game puts asymmetry in the decks and keeps the information open.

## Rubric (24/30)
Demand 4, Gap 3, Originality 4, Producibility 4, Simulatability 4, Owner fit 5.

## Constraints for the designer
- Components: 40 cards (20 per side) plus 1 token or 9 track cards. No dice, no board.
- Play time 15 min (simulated length within +/-20%).
- KPIs: seat/side win gap of 5 points or less (the main risk, since asymmetric sides are hard to balance); strategic bot beats random by 20 points or more; at least 2 lead changes per game; zero dead cards.
- Each side's special rules must fit on half an A5 card; no more than 3 unique keywords per side.
- Sides must be codable as simple rule modules so bots can test both.
- Differs from Duel Flip: no shared river, no push-your-luck.

## Red flag
Asymmetric balance. Two different decks rarely land within 5 points without several tuning passes, so budget for revision loops. Gap evidence is also moderate and unverified on BGG.

Candidates considered: Tug of Crowns (24), All-at-Once Draft (23), Nine Fields (24).
