# Brief: Silent Duo

- **Working title / slug:** Silent Duo / `silent-duo-deduction-coop`
- **Idea-bank id:** `silent-duo-deduction-coop` (from scan 2026-10-03)

## Target
- Players: 2-3 (best at 2). Play time: 20 min (cap 25). Audience: couples, pairs and friends who like The Crew, Sky Team and Bomb Busters. Ages 10+. Complexity: 2.5/5.

## Core mechanics and theme
- Co-operative deduction with strict limited communication: the only signal is which card you play, and when. No talking about your hand.
- The team must jointly work out a hidden number sequence (or sequences) before a fog/timer track runs out.
- Theme direction: lighthouse keepers signalling across fog. The theme justifies the silence, and light and fog give a natural timer.

## Market gap
- Limited-communication co-op is proven. Bomb Busters has a BGG geek rating of 7.4 (average 8.0, about 7,750 ratings) and about 13,900 logged plays by March 2026. Sky Team is about 7.9, a 2-player game of about 20 minutes.
- Co-op articles praise restricted talk because it avoids the "quarterback problem" (hexagamers 2026 co-op list).
- The gap is a card-only, tuckbox-sized, 20-minute 2-3 player version. Bomb Busters needs many tiles and wire components. Sky Team needs a board and dice. The Crew is trick-taking, not deduction.
- Evidence limits: ratings come from retailer and aggregator pages, not BGG directly. The Crew's rating was not confirmed in this check (the bank notes about 7.8).

## Comparables
1. **Bomb Busters** (7.4 geek): deduction through restricted talk. Silent Duo uses only cards, with no talking at all, and finishes faster.
2. **The Crew** (about 7.8): shared mission, no table talk. Silent Duo deduces a hidden sequence; it does not win tricks.
3. **Sky Team** (about 7.9): 2-player, 20 minutes, limited sharing. Silent Duo has no board or dice and scales to 3.

## Rubric (25/30)
Demand 4, Gap 3, Originality 4, Producibility 5, Simulatability 4, Owner fit 5.

## Constraints for the designer
- Components: one deck of 60 cards or fewer, plus a few tokens at most. No board, no dice.
- Play time 20 minutes; simulated length within +/-20%.
- Rules must be fully codeable: define a communication model (what a played card can legally signal) so bots can play without free talk.
- Both signals and silence must carry information; avoid solutions where one player dictates every play.
- Check that a silent team can win at 40-60% by strategy, and that a random team wins much less (gap of 20 points or more).
- Zero dead cards and zero rule ambiguities at pitch. Make seat balance irrelevant (symmetric roles) or within 5 points.

## Risks
- Crowded niche with strong incumbents; the originality must come from the card-as-signal mechanism.
- "Communicate only by the card played" can collapse into a fixed code the players agree in advance. The design needs to keep this from being solved.
