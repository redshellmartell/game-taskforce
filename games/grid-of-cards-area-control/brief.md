# Brief: Nine Fields

- **Working title / slug:** Nine Fields / `grid-of-cards-area-control`
- **Idea-bank id:** `grid-of-cards-area-control` (scan 2026-10-03)

## Target
- Players: 2-4
- Play time: 20 minutes (cap 25)
- Audience: ages 10+, hobby-light players who like short abstract-ish strategy
- Complexity: 2.5 / 5

## Core mechanics and theme
- Area control on a 3x3 grid of nine island cards. Players place a few pawns per turn on islands and take control by majority.
- The grid changes during play: islands flip, and whole rows or columns shift, so a lead can move or vanish. The twist to design is the flip/shift trigger. It should be tied to placement and visible to all, so play is tactical and not random.
- Theme: shifting islands (tides, drifting archipelago).

## Market gap
Demand is moderate, not proven. Modular-map designs appear among emerging mechanics in a 2026 hot-list summary (csr.hdsupply.com/best-board-games-2026). Micro area-control on a tiny grid is small but real: Contactics (itch.io, 3x3 area control) and Collapsi (shifting rows and columns) show designers exploring it. The closest published match is Shifting Stones (nine tiles in a 3x3 grid that players manipulate), but it is pattern-matching, not area control. BGG could not be opened, so ratings are unverified.

## Comparables and difference
- **Shifting Stones:** 3x3 manipulated grid, but secret-pattern scoring. Nine Fields scores open majority control with 3-4 players.
- **Onitama:** shared shifting options, 2 players. Nine Fields adds multiplayer area scoring.
- **Res Arcana / Love Letter-class small boxes:** Nine Fields is lighter, and the cards are the board.

## Rubric (max 30)
Demand 3, Gap 3, Originality 4, Producibility 5, Simulatability 4, Owner fit 5. **Total 24.**

## Constraints for the designer
- Under 40 cards plus 12-16 pawns or cubes. No other components.
- Rules fit on one page. Turn takes under 30 seconds.
- Grid shift/flip rules must be deterministic and bot-simulable.
- Check seat balance in 2, 3 and 4 player games; watch for kingmaking at 3-4 players and for runaway leaders.
- Aim for at least 2 lead changes per game.
