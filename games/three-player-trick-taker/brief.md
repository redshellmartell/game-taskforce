# Brief: Three-Hand Trick-Taker

- **Working title / slug:** Three-Hand Trick-Taker / `three-player-trick-taker`
- **Idea-bank id:** `three-player-trick-taker` (scan of 2026-10-03)

## Target
- **Players:** exactly 3 (no 2 or 4+ mode)
- **Play time:** 20 minutes (cap 25)
- **Audience:** adults and families (10+), who know basic trick-taking
- **Complexity:** 2 / 5

## Core mechanics and theme
- Trick-taking in which each seat has a different **role** each round: **leader** (sets the target), **chaser** (tries to match it) and **spoiler** (wins by denying). Roles rotate every round, so all three seats get the same turns over a full game (3, 6 or 9 rounds).
- Theme direction: a heist crew splitting a haul. Tricks are the job, and roles are the planner, the safecracker and the double-crosser.

## Market gap
- Three is the awkward player count for card games. Most lists name a handful of old classics (Hearts, Sheepshead, Skat) that need house rules or card removal, and few recent designs target exactly 3 (destructoid best-3-player list).
- Trick-taking is hot. The Crew (BGG 7.8, rank about #96, best at 4, so 3 is not its sweet spot) and Skull King (BGG average 7.5, about 14,000 ratings) show lasting demand. Fox in the Forest (7.1, 18,000 ratings) is 2-player only.
- Demand is real but not exceptional (scored 3): this is a niche-in-a-popular-genre play, not a trend riding wave.

## Comparables
1. **The Crew** (BGG 7.8): co-op, scales 2-5, best at 4. This is competitive, tuned for 3 only.
2. **Skull King** (7.5): bidding, 2-8 players, with chaotic scoring. This has asymmetric roles and no bidding, with more deterministic play.
3. **Hearts / Sheepshead (3-player)**: need house rules. This is built for 3 from the start, with rotating roles that fix the 2-vs-1 gang-up problem.

## Rubric scores (26/30)
Demand 3, Gap 4, Originality 4, Producibility 5, Simulatability 5, Owner fit 5.

## Constraints for the designer
- A standard-size deck only: at most 40 cards, no board, no tokens beyond a score pad or a few counters.
- Rules must fit one page. No more than 3 special rules in total.
- Over a full rotation each seat must play each role equally often. **Seat balance gap 5 points or less** is the main risk and must be tested first.
- Avoid kingmaking: the spoiler role must not be able to hand the game to one rival.
- Target at least 2 lead changes per game and a runaway leader rate of 65% or less.
- Simulated length within 20% of 20 minutes (16-24).
