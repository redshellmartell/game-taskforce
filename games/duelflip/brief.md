# Brief: Duel Flip (slug: duelflip)

## Target
- Players: 2 (designed for 2, not scaled down), optional 3-4
- Play time: 10-15 min
- Audience: casual/gateway couples, commuters, Flip 7 and Sea Salt & Paper fans
- Complexity: 1.5/5 (rules in under 5 minutes)

## Core mechanics and theme
- Push-your-luck on a shared, visible draw row: both players draw from the same river of cards, so pushing also feeds your opponent information and options.
- Banking vs. busting, with a "bust" that hands the opponent the cards you flipped.
- Theme direction: rival tide-pool or night-market scavengers. Light, abstract-friendly, no heavy art needs.

## Market gap
- Push-your-luck card games are at peak demand. Flip 7 is described as the most popular game of the past year, driven by social media (Rain City Games, Summer 2026 hits). Sea Salt & Paper and Courtisans are also listed as hits.
- Flip 7 is praised for a 5-minute teach and 2-minute setup, but reviewers dislike its lack of a dedicated 2P mode and heavy luck (hexagamers.com, whatsericplaying.com reviews). Take-that swings are a common complaint.
- Card games fund strongly: Gamefound reports +66% funding for Invincible: The Card Game after moving from Kickstarter, and Cyberpunk TCG raised over $28M (Gamefound case study, Kickstarter and BoardGameWire coverage).
- Caveat: gap evidence is thin. I could not open the Rain City or Gamefound pages (blocked by network), so I relied on search summaries. I did not view the live BGG hot list or check BGG ratings. The 2P-specific claim rests on a few reviews and needs the designer's own check against BGG 2P rankings.

## Comparables
- Flip 7: great teach speed. Differ by making 2P the primary mode, using shared information, and cutting random take-that.
- Sea Salt & Paper: set collection with duo cards. Differ by being faster and push-your-luck rather than drafting.
- Skull / Cacti-style bluffers: Differ by using no hidden bluffing, only skill in risk management.

## Constraints for the designer
- Components: one deck of at most 60-70 cards, plus optional score tokens. No boards, dice, or custom pieces.
- Play time cap: 15 min. Setup under 1 minute.
- Rules must fit on one page. Luck must be mitigated by visible information, not by take-that.
- Keep the state small and deterministic, so it can be bot-simulated (target: thousands of games for balance, check first-player advantage).
- Must be distinct from Flip 7: no copying its 7-unique-number win condition.
- Target retail price about $10-15.
