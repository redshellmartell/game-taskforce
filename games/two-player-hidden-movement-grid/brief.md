# Brief: Dead Reckoning

- **Working title / slug:** Dead Reckoning / `two-player-hidden-movement-grid`
- **Idea-bank id:** `two-player-hidden-movement-grid` (scan of 2026-10-03; written from the bank, no new searches)

## Target
- **Players:** 2 (built for two, not scaled down)
- **Play time:** 15 minutes (cap 20)
- **Audience:** adults and families (10+) who like quick abstract duels and bluffing
- **Complexity:** 2 / 5

## Core mechanics and theme
- Both players secretly plan a **three-step path** on paper across a **5x5 grid of face-down cards**. The two plans are revealed and resolved together, step by step, so each player must predict the other's moves, not react to them.
- The grid is just cards laid out on the table, not a board. Cards flip as they are entered, so the fog slowly lifts and shared knowledge builds up.
- Theme direction: two submarines in a fog bank, plotting courses by dead reckoning, with cards as sea squares (hazards, supplies, targets).
- Success means each round has a real rock-paper-scissors layer (aim for where the opponent will be, avoid collisions) with meaningful card effects, not a coin flip.

## Market gap
- Two-player focus is an identified 2026 trend (boardgamequest 2026 mid-year list, as banked), and downtime is the most repeated complaint in review search results (gnomestew). Simultaneous planning removes waiting.
- Search results of the 2-player card catalogue (boardgameshop.nl) show many duels but no hidden-movement grid game.
- Demand scored 3: evidence is trend-level from search summaries; no sales data, and the banked source pages could not be opened.

## Comparables
1. **Onitama** (BGG rating not found): alternating, fully open, deterministic moves. This one plans in secret and resolves at once.
2. **Battleship** (rating not found): guessing on a fixed hidden map. This has movement, collisions and a map that changes as it is revealed.
3. **Nine Fields (banked, in pipeline)**: also a grid of cards. Keep it distinct: this is 2-player, hidden planning, with no area control.

Demand 3, Gap 3, Originality 4, Producibility 5, Simulatability 5, Owner fit 5.

## Constraints for the designer
- Components: 25 grid cards plus at most 20 small extra cards (path or action cards if needed), pencil and paper (or two small pads) for plans. No board, no miniatures; tokens optional and few.
- Rules fit one page, with at most 3 special rules. Set-up under 2 minutes.
- Plans must resolve deterministically from the written path, so bots can simulate every game.
- Seat balance gap 5 points or less (check start positions and who wins ties); strategic-vs-random win gap 20 points or more; runaway leader rate 65% or less; at least 2 lead changes per game.
- Simulated length within 20% of 15 minutes (12-18). No dominant "always do X" path; every card type must matter (zero dead cards).
- Collision and tie rules must be unambiguous (zero rule ambiguities).
- Main risk: simultaneous guessing can feel like pure luck. Test that skill (reading the opponent, using revealed cards) beats random play by the KPI margin.
