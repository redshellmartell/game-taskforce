aspects based on comparable games on the market in following way:

Across the design audit, the recurring comparisons were:

Bang! — the closest comp, cited repeatedly: its character-count asymmetry (16 characters) vs. our 12 Stars, its near-binary HP distribution, and its combat-dense card pool vs. ours being spread across more categories.
Smash Up — the closest structural cousin for hand economy: no mana curve, card-count-gated plays rather than resource-gated, which is why it beat MTG/Dominion as the reference point for draw/play ratio.
Fluxx and Exploding Kittens — cited for keeping personal turn economy flat across player counts rather than scaling it with table size.
Sushi Go — cited for the 108-card print target (matches its print run, plus clean base-12 sheet economics).
MTG and Dominion — mentioned but explicitly rejected as comps, since both are resource-gated systems, unlike Heavenly Bodies' no-mana design.
Love Letter, Coup (smaller decks) and Munchkin (larger) — used as bracketing reference points when the ~90–100 card draw-pile estimate was first being sized.
