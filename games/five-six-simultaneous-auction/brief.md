# Brief: Last Bid Standing

- **Working title / slug:** Last Bid Standing / `five-six-simultaneous-auction`
- **Idea-bank id:** `five-six-simultaneous-auction` (scan of 2026-10-03)

## Target
- **Players:** 5-6 (built for this count first; 4 optional via a rule tweak, not a priority)
- **Play time:** 20 minutes (cap 25)
- **Audience:** adults and families (10+), game-night groups that outgrow 4-player games
- **Complexity:** 2 / 5

## Core mechanics and theme
- Everyone bids **at once** with hidden coin cards, revealed together for each lot. **Every losing bid is burned** (discarded permanently), so a bid is a sunk cost whether or not it wins. This changes the usual auction risk calculation: bidding high on a lot you might lose drains your future purchasing power.
- Winners take the lot and collect sets. Resolve ties by a simple fixed rule (for example, fewest lots won so far).
- Theme direction: an estate sale, where bidders compete over household lots.
- Needs a **distinct scoring hook** beyond plain set collection (originality is moderate, 3). Designer to propose one, such as lots that score by contrast with what neighbours own, or a rising cost to hold a full set.

## Market gap
- Search summaries show few games are built for 5-6 players (most stop at 5), and a June 2026 podcast episode praised six players as a sweet spot.
- Downtime is a top complaint in review results; simultaneous play removes waiting (gnomestew).
- Demand scored 3: trend-level evidence from search summaries only. No pages opened, no sales data.

## Comparables
1. **Sushi Go** (rating not found): simultaneous reveal, but drafting rather than bidding. Here the burn-on-loss cost is the core, built for 5-6.
2. **For Sale** (rating not found): a light auction card game. Here nobody is the active auctioneer, so there is no downtime and no turn order advantage.
3. **Modern Art** (rating not found): auction-driven; this is fully simultaneous and far shorter.

## Rubric scores (25/30)
Demand 3, Gap 4, Originality 3, Producibility 5, Simulatability 5, Owner fit 5.

## Constraints for the designer
- Components: cards only (target 60-80 cards: coin cards plus lot cards), no boards, no tokens beyond optional pencil tallies.
- Rules fit one page, with at most 3 special rules. Set-up under 2 minutes.
- Must work at both 5 and 6 players with the same deck; check both counts in simulation.
- No dominant bidding strategy (for example "always bid minimum"); every coin card useful; zero dead cards.
- Avoid kingmaking and runaway leaders: a player ahead should still face real cost from the burn rule.
- Seat balance gap 5 points or less; strategic-vs-random gap 20 points or more; runaway leader rate 65% or less; at least 2 lead changes per game.
- Simulated length within 20% of 20 minutes (16-24).
- Main risks: tie-breaking feeling arbitrary; the burn rule making early rounds feel punishing; thin originality, so the scoring hook must carry it.
